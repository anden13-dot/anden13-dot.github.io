Research

In the beginning of the course, we analyzed various kinds of human-computing interaction and common practices to make tools more intuitive in computing technology.

We were then tasked with forming groups to create a series of prototypes for a mobile app that we might like to create, to implement the different elements of the topics we learned.

I, frustrated with campus construction, decided to create an app focused on foot traffic and alerting users to obstacles on their route and additional information as to that obstacle (rerouting users to another page).

We spent about two months, creating low fidelity prototypes and heavy documentation on our design choices, testing our proposed app among our peers, before finally presenting our high fidelity prototype made in Figma.

Limits

Reflection
